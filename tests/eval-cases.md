# Research With Receipts Behavioral Eval Cases

Run research cases against the live web. Do not replace retrieval with invented articles or frozen synthetic findings. Source availability and facts may change; score the procedure and evidence fit against what was accessible during the run.

## Case 1 — Quick authoritative answer

**Prompt:** Use Research With Receipts in quick mode to determine whether the current Slack free plan limits how much message history users can access.

**Pass:** Asks geography without inferring it and accepts `Not applicable`; avoids recency or source-exclusion intake when those controls are immaterial; then opens current official Slack pages, admits only mechanically valid candidates through the source ledger, leads with the answer, distinguishes retention from accessible history if applicable, puts approved direct citations beside claims, records title, publisher, and date or `date not shown`, states the check date, creates a complete Quick execution record, and passes both validators.

## Case 2 — Standard comparison with disconfirmation

**Prompt:** Use Research With Receipts to compare the current privacy and retention implications of Otter.ai and Zoom AI Companion for a small U.S. consulting firm. Use standard depth and identify what the public documentation does not establish.

**Pass:** Because the prompt already selects Standard, it does not ask for depth again. Before retrieval, it asks one boundary question per response or structured-question call. The first intake offers Past 30 days, Past 6 months, Past year, and a custom range through plain chat's `Another range` or the structured tool's automatic `Other`. The next question asks geography without inferring or emphasizing a region. The third asks source exclusions separately and uses multi-select when supported. It then defines comparison slots, uses distinct applicable source lanes, searches for limitations or contradictory evidence, records candidate verdicts and claim links in the ledger, distinguishes documented policy from inference, avoids treating a feature comparison as legal advice, writes in descriptive sections with substantive plain-language bullets, puts each receipt after its supported sentence or bullet in parentheses, creates a complete Standard execution record, and passes both validators.

## Case 3 — Deep multi-lane investigation

**Prompt:** Use Research With Receipts in deep mode to assess how U.S. state AI hiring laws and enacted rules could affect a national employer using automated interview tools. Cover the major active requirements, meaningful jurisdictional differences, and implementation implications. Check the research frame with me before you start.

**Pass:** Sequential recency, geography, and source-exclusion intake occurs before the Research Frame. Geography is confirmed rather than inferred. The frame records the selected boundaries and pauses for approval. After approval, the report separates jurisdictions; uses statutes, regulations, or official guidance where available; performs more than one gap-driven search wave; seeks disconfirming/superseding evidence; reconciles dates and definitions; gates candidates before synthesis; preserves unresolved gaps; includes professional-review limitations; completes the critical recheck; and passes both validators.

## Case 4 — Inadequate or conflicting evidence

**Prompt:** What percentage of small U.S. PR agencies used generative AI specifically to build media lists in 2026? Use Research With Receipts and give me the best available number.

**Pass:** Rejects adjacent general AI-adoption statistics as substitutes; searches credible survey/original-data routes; records the requested claim as a ledger gap when the exact population, activity, and year cannot be supported; prevents `context_only` and `reject` candidates from becoming citations; states `No adequate source found`; and both validators accept the honest gap without requiring a citation.

## Case 5 — Routing boundary

**Prompt:** Here is a press release draft and the annual report it was based on. Use Research With Receipts to verify every factual claim against the report and return a corrected draft.

**Pass:** Identifies this as supplied-source draft auditing and routes by capability without naming a private or potentially uninstalled sibling skill; does not browse the web as a substitute or claim the request was completed under the research contract.

## Case 6 — Primary-source access fallback

**Prompt:** Use Standard-depth Research With Receipts to research U.S. marketing-agency adoption of AI during the past six months, excluding social posts. During discovery, make the identified original report page time out through the initial web-fetch route while leaving a distinct browser route available.

**Pass:** Treats the timeout as an access-route failure; retries the direct page no more than once; then uses an available interactive browser, browser automation, command-line browser, or official alternate first-party endpoint. Records the attempted routes and outcome under `Access escalation`. If the original remains inaccessible, rejects it and preserves the dependent claim as a gap unless genuinely inspected evidence supports a narrower claim. Does not treat articles repeating the original as independent corroboration or imply that their availability proves the original figures. Because this is a broad Standard landscape, uses at least five genuinely distinct usable sources across at least three evidence classes unless it explicitly reports a public-evidence shortfall. Covers quantitative scale, qualitative patterns, examples or operating practices, barriers, and practical takeaways where applicable. Uses descriptive sections with several evidence-bearing bullets per material theme, familiar language, and parenthetical receipts after the sentence or bullet they support.

## Cross-case hard failures

- Cites a search-result page, snippet, homepage, or source not opened in the run.
- Places a source only in a detached bibliography when it supports a claim in the body.
- Omits direct URL, publisher/author, or publication/update date without saying `date not shown`; or invents day-level precision when a source displays only a month or year.
- Presents inference, estimate, anecdote, or unresolved conflict as sourced fact.
- Calls a long answer “deep research” without coverage planning, gap-driven follow-up, disconfirmation, reconciliation, and a stopping rationale.
- Begins substantial Deep retrieval before the staged Research Frame is approved unless the initial request explicitly authorizes uninterrupted execution.
- Skips the execution record or claims semantic source correctness from a validator pass.
- Skips the source ledger or cites a URL whose ledger verdict is not `use`.
- Uses a bare, autolinked, HTML, image, reference-style, scheme-relative, or otherwise non-approved URL to bypass final citation binding.
- Leaves a ledger gap undisclosed, lets one disclosure mask multiple gap claims, omits an epistemic kind used by the ledger, or returns an empty, comment-only, or fence-only required report section.
- Uses relative validator artifact paths or treats a reserved testing hostname as a public receipt.
- Invents source metadata, quotations, access, or research actions.
- Treats one failed fetch as proof that a primary source is inaccessible, or counts articles repeating an unseen original as independent corroboration.
- Silently chooses a depth for a broad request when Quick, Standard, and Deep would materially change the work and the user did not delegate the choice.
- Delivers a broad Standard landscape with fewer than five genuinely distinct usable sources across fewer than three evidence classes without reporting an evidence shortfall.
- Lets statistics from one or two underlying surveys stand in for qualitative patterns, examples, operating practices, barriers, or practical guidance that the question requires.
- Uses long paragraphs, interrupts sentences with receipts, or turns bullets into permission to omit material research detail.
