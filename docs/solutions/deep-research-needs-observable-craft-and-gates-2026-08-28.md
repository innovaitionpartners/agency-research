# Deep research needs observable craft and gates

## Problem

The initial skill had strong source-quality and citation rules, but its Deep mode named desired behaviors without teaching or exposing the central work. “Use distinct search lanes,” “run disconfirming research,” and “recheck important claims” could all be skipped while the final report still looked complete.

The output templates checked presentation, not whether the promised research process happened. Deep planning was also described inside the same response path as execution, so it was easy to compress into a perfunctory preamble.

## Resolution

The hardened architecture uses four complementary controls:

1. A staged Research Frame is the entire first Deep response unless the user explicitly authorizes uninterrupted execution.
2. A focused reference teaches how to convert answer slots into Authority, Application, Challenge, Scope, and Freshness lanes, including how to adapt after weak results.
3. A structured execution record makes coverage, corroboration, disconfirmation, conflicts, gaps, rechecking, and the stopping decision observable before delivery.
4. A narrow validator checks deterministic answer and record surfaces while explicitly leaving source truth, applicability, page inspection, and claim support to semantic review.

Optional parallel research uses a portable evidence-lane brief. Workers return provenance records rather than report prose; the coordinator retains verification and synthesis.

## Maintainer rule

When expanding a research skill from lightweight answers to Deep research, do not treat source-quality reminders as the research method. The skill must teach the quality-bearing discovery craft, create a real phase boundary where planning matters, and expose enough execution state that skipped work is detectable. Deterministic validation should enforce shape only and must never certify semantic source quality.
